MindMora: Billiard Memory (Android package com.mindmora.app) is published by Sukura Tech. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game. For privacy questions or requests, contact suker.alashraf@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

MindMora saves your progress, stars, scores, gameplay statistics, selected appearances, reward eligibility and preferences on your device. Preferences include language, sound, vibration and optional reminder settings. We use this information to restore your game, apply your choices and manage rewards. The game does not require an account, and Sukura Tech does not operate a server that receives your saved gameplay progress.

Android may back up or transfer saved progress and preferences, depending on your device, account and backup settings. These backups are managed by Android and your backup provider, rather than a MindMora cloud-save service.

Advertising and Google services

MindMora integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for optional rewarded advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform for privacy messages and consent handling. Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional; receiving its reward depends on the advertisement reporting that the reward was earned.

Google's advertising services can collect and share information including your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. An IP address can be used to estimate your approximate location. This information is used for advertising, ad measurement and analytics, service operation, and fraud prevention. The data handled depends on the service configuration, available identifiers, your choices and applicable requirements.

Privacy-status checks and, when permitted, advertisement preloading can contact Google before you choose to watch an advertisement. Playing without watching an advertisement does not necessarily prevent these connections. Google may process information in countries other than your own, as described in its privacy policy.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising data disclosure for details about Google's processing. Android provides advertising privacy controls, including advertising identifier controls where available. The options and their location depend on your Android version and device. Privacy messages presented by the game allow choices where required and supported by Google's configured service.

Permissions and reminders

If you enable daily reminders, MindMora uses your chosen reminder time to schedule a local notification. You can turn reminders off in the game or manage notification permission in Android settings. The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise device location for gameplay. Approximate location inferred by advertising services from an IP address is described above.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments or other information you choose to provide. We use this information to answer your request, provide support and address privacy or security issues. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store these messages. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Visiting this policy page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without a GitHub account. For information about GitHub's handling of website-visitor data, see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or cookies to this policy page.

Sharing and security

Information is handled by the providers described above for advertising, consent, device backup and email delivery. We may disclose information we hold when necessary to comply with a legal obligation or protect users and the service. Sukura Tech does not sell personal information.

We use Android's private app storage for game data and integrity protections for important progress records. Google's advertising SDK uses encryption in transit. No storage or transmission method provides an absolute security guarantee.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains until it is reset, cleared or removed. The in-game reset clears gameplay progress but retains some preferences and reward-related records. To remove the app's local data completely, use Android's Clear storage option or uninstall the game. Android backups may remain or restore data later; manage them separately through your device and backup-account settings.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to deal with the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Google's handling and retention of advertising and consent information are described in its policies and controls. Clearing the game's local storage does not itself delete information already processed by Google or copies held in device backups.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact suker.alashraf@gmail.com about information held by Sukura Tech. We may need to verify a request. We cannot retrieve or remotely erase a saved game that is only stored on your device. For information held by Google or your backup provider, use that provider's privacy controls or request channels. MindMora does not create user accounts, so there is no game account to delete.

Young users

MindMora is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and over, and is not directed to children under 13. The game does not currently ask you to provide your age or date of birth. The meaning of a child and rules about consent vary by country. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information relating to a child, contact us so we can review the concern and take appropriate action concerning information we hold.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for a change.
